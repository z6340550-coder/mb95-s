About MB95

A founder-led technology company from Malerkotla, Punjab, building an independent, integrated technology ecosystem.

Why MB95 exists

Most technology competes for attention. MB95 builds products that work as coherent environments instead: each one has a clear job, and together they help a person move from an objective to the next action.

Our decisions start from clear user needs, system behaviour people can understand, and development we can sustain.

A plan is more than a list of tasks

Our products are built around one model of how work gets done. A plan has six parts.

  1. Objective

    The outcome you are pursuing.

  2. Requirements

    The skills, resources and conditions you need.

  3. Actions and habits

    One-time actions and repeated behaviours that move the work forward.

  4. Behaviour

    Ways to handle procrastination, consistency, attention and decisions.

  5. Constraints

    Limits to respect, and what you deliberately leave out.

  6. Evaluation

    The evidence that shows whether the approach is working.
