WorkShell

Your phone and tablet, organised into Shells: a focused environment for each kind of work.

Get release updates

How WorkShell works

Each Shell is built for one kind of use, so your phone opens to what you came to do.

  • A Shell for each focus

    Social, Memory, Explore and General, each its own environment.

  • Social management

    Keep track of the people in your life and how you stay in touch.

  • Essentials first

    Camera, recorder, phone and messages are always one tap away, plus one slot you choose.

  • Separate by default

    Data in each Shell stays apart unless you choose to share it.

  • No attention traps

    Designed to help you finish things, not keep you scrolling.

  • Phones and tablets

    One system across both, and it works with Workdesk on your desktop.

Today and next

Today

WorkShell works as a launcher on Android phones and tablets.

Next

WorkShell becomes an independent mobile operating system for phones and tablets.
