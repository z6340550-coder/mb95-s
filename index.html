The ecosystem

Three products with distinct responsibilities. They connect where it helps, and each one stays useful on its own.

  • Workdesk

    In development

    A desktop operating environment for people who want structure in their work. It organises plans, tasks and reviews into focused workspaces, and gives quick access to the applications that already do specialised jobs well.

    • Plans, tasks, milestones and habits in one place
    • Reminders, constraints and progress evaluation
    • Separate workspaces for reading, writing, thinking and general use
    • For students, developers, writers, researchers and entrepreneurs

  • Workshell

    In development

    A launcher for Android phones and tablets. Instead of a grid of unrelated apps, Workshell groups your apps into Shells, each built for one kind of use. It works on its own, and with Workdesk for shared plans and tasks.

    • Dark theme by default

    Planned

A plan is more than a list of tasks

Our products are built around one model of how work gets done. A plan is a structure for making decisions, and it has six parts.

  1. Objective

    The outcome you are pursuing.

  2. Requirements

    The skills, resources and conditions you need.

  3. Actions and habits

    One-time actions and repeated behaviours that move the work forward.

  4. Behaviour

    Ways to handle procrastination, consistency, attention and decisions.

  5. Constraints

    Limits to respect, and what you deliberately leave out.

  6. Evaluation

    The evidence that shows whether the approach is working.

We support your own way of working. We don't assume everyone needs the same routine.
