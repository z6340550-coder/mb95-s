Workdesk

A desktop operating environment for people who want structure in their work.

Get release updates Pricing

What Workdesk does

Workdesk organises your work instead of competing for your attention. It gives quick access to the applications that already do specialised jobs well.

  • Plans and tasks

    Objectives, milestones, tasks and habits in one place.

  • Focused workspaces

    Separate spaces for reading, writing, thinking and general use.

  • Progress you can see

    Reminders and evaluation show whether the approach is working.

  • Private by design

    Your notes and journal stay on your computer, encrypted.

  • Workdesk Store

    Add workspaces, looks and add-ons when you need them.

  • Built on Linux

    A complete system you install on your PC, not an app on top of one.

Who it is for

Students, developers, writers, researchers and entrepreneurs: anyone who wants their computer to support a plan rather than a stream of distractions.

What you need

  • A 64-bit PC that starts in UEFI mode
  • A disk of at least 16 GB that can be erased
  • An internet connection for setup and updates
